What it does

Everything your Pit needs to steal the show.

01 · MOTION

It looks at you

Head swivel, head tilt, hip and arm, smooth and independent. Two spare channels for whatever you add next.

02 · POWER

Smooth, every scene

Motion and brain each run on their own dedicated power, so every move is smooth and steady, show after show.

03 · LIGHT

Eyes that glow

Drive a 7-LED RGB jewel for eyes or a chest glow, in any color you like. Works great with the Adafruit NeoPixel Jewel (7 LED), or any 7-LED WS2812-compatible jewel.

04 · SOUND

A chirp for every scene

Plays clips from a microSD card: chitters, beeps, warbles, whatever your Pit has to say. Your first skit is waiting on the quick-start page.

05 · SMART HOME

Every notification becomes a skit

Built on ESPHome for Home Assistant. Laundry done? A happy chirp. Garage open too long? An urgent warble and a slow head turn. Or run it standalone.

06 · READY

Plug in and play

Tool-free 12 V lever terminal and built-in protection. Connect, power up, and it's showtime.
