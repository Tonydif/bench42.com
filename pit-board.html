What it does

Everything a pit character needs to come alive.

01 · MOTION

Four servos, two spares

Head swivel, head tilt, hip and arm — smooth and independent. Two extra channels for whatever you add next.

02 · POWER

Servo spikes don't cause reboots

The servos get their own 5 A supply, separate from the brain. A stalled arm won't take the whole character down.

03 · LIGHT

Light ring output

Drive a NeoPixel (WS2812) ring for eyes or a chest glow, in any color you like.

04 · SOUND

Your own sound clips

Plays clips from a microSD card. Bring your own sounds — or make new ones.

05 · SMART HOME

Home Assistant ready

Built on ESPHome. Trigger moves, sounds and light from your automations — or run it standalone.

06 · PROTECTED

Forgiving by design

Reverse-polarity protection and a resettable fuse on the 12 V input, for the mistakes everyone makes once.
